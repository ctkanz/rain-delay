MILWAUKEE CRAFTERS • BOOK ONE

RAIN DELAY

BY SARAH WITHERSPOON

✦
“Sometimes in baseball, you get a rain delay. You wait it out, you bide your time, and when the weather clears, you step up to the plate and swing.”

Playing for your hometown team is every baseball player's dream.

Some could pen me as the Golden Boy for the Milwaukee Crafters. The face of the team. The all-star. The one all future baseball players will look up to.

The team has assigned me a social media manager for the season to not only help maintain my image, but give Milwaukee the proper recognition it deserves.

Which, fine. No big deal.

That's until I find out who's going to be managing all of that for me this season—Eliana Romano. The girl whose heart I broke five years ago.

There can be no distractions.
Everything must remain professional.
Strictly business.
Everything by the books.

But one thing was for sure, I was willing to bend some of the rules to make Eliana mine again.
